Cloud-native teams are drowning in their own telemetry, with research showing roughly half of collected metrics are never queried or acted upon. The article lays out strategies for sustainable observability pipelines, including an "observability mesh" interconnecting traces, metrics, logs, and profiles, balancing zero-code versus manual instrumentation, smart sampling, and managing high cardinality. It also covers preparing observability for AI-driven architectures where success shifts from system uptime to decision quality.
